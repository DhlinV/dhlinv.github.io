Honglin He

I am a second-year PhD student at the Department of Computer Science, University of California, Los Angeles, advised by Prof. Bolei Zhou in the Vision and Autonomy Intelligence Lab (VAIL).

I obtained my Master (2021.09 - 2024.06) at the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yi Zhang and Prof. Shuo Feng. Before that, I received my B.Eng (2017.09 - 2021.06) at the Department of Automation, Xiamen University.

My research lies at the intersection of Embodied AI, Urban Simulation, and Robot Navigation. I build scalable simulation platforms and learning-based navigation policies that help robots move safely through the sidewalks and public spaces people share every day.
