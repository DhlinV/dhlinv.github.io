I am a second-year CS PhD student at UCLA, advised by Prof. <a href="https://boleizhou.github.io/">Bolei Zhou</a> in the <a href="https://vail-ucla.github.io/">Vision and Autonomy Intelligence Lab (VAIL)</a>.

I received my M.S. (2024) in Automation from Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1076/3242.htm">Yi Zhang</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1076/3247.htm">Shuo Feng</a>, and my B.Eng. (2021) in Automation from Xiamen University.

My research spans <strong>Embodied AI</strong>, <strong>Urban Simulation</strong>, and <strong>Robot Navigation</strong>. I build scalable simulators and learned navigation policies that help robots move safely on urban sidewalks.
